# CodexBar 1.2 release notes

## App Store “What's New” candidate

CodexBar 1.2 is our biggest update yet.

- Bring your usage to Apple Watch with a live dashboard and configurable
  complications.
- Make the dashboard yours: reorder, resize, restyle, show, hide, and collapse
  account metrics with a live card editor.
- Track more services, including OpenCode Go limits, Moonshot (Kimi) balances,
  and completed Greptile reviews.
- Connect multiple ChatGPT / Codex accounts with private, independent browser
  sign-in.
- Set separate Warning and Critical Alert thresholds for current and projected
  usage.
- Find accounts, widgets, recovery, and private feedback faster in the new
  Settings and Support experience.

This release also improves widget freshness, Watch syncing, browser sign-in,
usage history, account recovery, accessibility, and refresh reliability.

## Long-form customer release notes

CodexBar 1.2 makes it easier to see the usage that matters, arrange it around
the way you work, and keep it close on every Apple device.

### Your dashboard, your way

The new Customize Card editor gives you a live preview while you reorder,
resize, restyle, show, or hide individual metrics. Layouts adapt naturally to
the available width, and account cards can now collapse independently when you
want a more compact overview. Plan badges make verified ChatGPT / Codex,
Claude, and GitHub Copilot tiers easier to recognize at a glance.

### CodexBar comes to Apple Watch

Version 1.2 adds a live, read-only Apple Watch dashboard that follows your
iPhone card order and visualization choices while keeping the last good data
available when the phone is out of reach. New inline, circular, rectangular,
and corner complications surface usage, freshness, and warning or critical
status directly on the watch face. Per-metric controls let you decide what
appears on Watch without changing the iPhone dashboard.

### More providers and better account workflows

You can now track OpenCode Go's rolling 5-hour, weekly, and monthly limits next
to the OpenCode Zen balance, including reset times and trustworthy
current-pace projections. Moonshot (Kimi) joins the balance providers, and the
new Greptile integration shows account-scoped completed-review activity across
the dashboard, history, widgets, and Watch.

A single Add Account flow now covers every provider, and ChatGPT / Codex users
can connect additional identities with private, independent browser sessions.
Each dashboard card also has a direct Configure Account shortcut.

### Smarter alerts, widgets, and history

Warning and Critical Alert percentages can be configured separately and now
apply consistently to current and projected usage across the app, widgets,
Watch, and complications. Widgets keep the current percentage prominent,
report the age of the oldest data they actually show, and deep-link directly
to the matching account. Cursor history now reliably tracks Total usage by
default, with stable series for its other usage types.

### Clearer Settings and safer support

Settings is reorganized into focused destinations for Accounts, Dashboard,
Alerts, Widgets, Help, and Recovery. The new Feedback & Support hub offers
private, account-free email drafts, public GitHub forms, known issues, and the
support guide in one place. Optional diagnostics are previewable and designed
to exclude credentials, account labels, balances, usage history, and stored
Watch or widget snapshots before anything is shared.

### Reliability and accessibility throughout

This release includes extensive fixes for browser sign-in, account and
Keychain recovery, stale refresh results, Watch delivery, widget freshness,
history persistence, projection presentation, and provider-specific data. It
also improves VoiceOver explanations for hidden metrics that trigger warning
or critical card status.
