CodexBar 1.4.0 adds more provider metrics, guided sign-in, and longer usage history.

- Connect Google Gemini and see Gemini Apps, Gemini Models, and Other models in one account, with separate five-hour and weekly limits, reset times, and usage projections where available.
- Connect Grok to see verified paid weekly usage and Extra Usage Credits when available, separately from Cursor's Grok Bot allowance.
- Monitor GitHub Billing through its own sign-in, with personal and organization spending, included allowances, budgets, and month-end projections where supported.
- Connect OpenCode Go and Zen through browser approval and workspace selection, without pasting credentials.
- Choose browser or private sign-in for ChatGPT / Codex and OpenCode. Distinct Codex accounts stay separate, and duplicate identities remain blocked.
- Track every reported Codex usage window, including separate Codex Spark limits. Optionally show the reported Codex Credits balance, with a separate choice for each account.
- See Cursor Models, Other Models, and Grok Bot usage separately. Over-limit readings and on-demand spending stay visible. Expired Cursor sign-in renews when possible; guided reconnection preserves your saved card choices.
- Explore up to 90 days of daily usage history alongside recent detailed samples, with clearer dates at the chart edges on iPhone and iPad.
- See Greptile's provider-returned free-allowance renewal date through a verified dashboard session. Fresh Google-backed Greptile billing sign-in or reconnection may be unavailable in this version.

This update also clarifies Claude's five-hour and weekly windows and Codex's 30-day window, improves saved-account and History recovery, and makes account menus easier to open on iPad. Metrics depend on the connected provider and account; missing data is not replaced with guessed usage.
