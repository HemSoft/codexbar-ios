CodexBar 1.3 brings the complete 1.2 feature set to the App Store, plus clearer service health, history, and alert details.

- Bring your usage to Apple Watch with a live dashboard and configurable complications.
- Customize dashboard cards by reordering, resizing, restyling, showing, hiding, and collapsing metrics.
- Track OpenCode Go limits, Moonshot (Kimi) balances, and completed Greptile reviews.
- Connect multiple ChatGPT / Codex accounts with private, independent browser sign-in.
- Set separate Warning and Critical Alert thresholds. Notifications identify the provider, account, metric, value, and configured threshold.
- Opt in to GitHub service-status monitoring with accessible incident and recovery alerts.
- Choose how often regular refreshes add samples to History.
- See newly granted banked Codex resets and keep over-limit history visible above the 100% allowance line.

This release also improves Settings, support, widget freshness, Watch syncing, browser sign-in, account recovery, accessibility, and refresh reliability. GitHub status checks use GitHub's public endpoint without account credentials or provider usage data, and monitoring state stays on your device.
